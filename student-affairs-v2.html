STUDENT RIGHTS

What every student can expect

As part of the JIC community, these are the core standards and rights you can expect from Student Affairs every time.

Respect & dignity

Every student is welcomed with courtesy, listened to fully and treated as a partner, not a file number.

Confidentiality

What you share with a counselor or advisor stays private and is only used to help you.

Fairness for all

Rules apply equally to everyone. Decisions are explained clearly and based on the college regulations.

The right to be heard

You can raise a complaint, appeal a decision or review a grade through a clear, respectful process.

Timely responses

Requests are followed up and you are kept informed about where your case stands.

A safe campus

A learning environment free from harassment and discrimination, where you can focus on your goals.

FAQ

The questions students ask most

Can't find your answer? Visit the Student Affairs office and the staff will help you in person.

How do I add or drop a course?
You can add or drop courses through the JIC Student Portal during the official add/drop period at the start of the semester, or visit Admission & Registration with your academic advisor's approval.
What happens if I miss too many classes?
Exceeding the allowed absence limit without an approved excuse can result in a DN (Denial) grade for the course. Always submit valid medical or official excuses promptly to Student Affairs.
Can I postpone a semester?
Yes, eligible students may apply to defer (postpone) a semester before the published deadline in the academic calendar through the Student Portal or Admission & Registration office.
How do I get an official letter or enrolment certificate?
You can request official identification letters and enrolment certificates directly from the Student Portal or by visiting the Letters & Documents desk at Student Affairs with your student ID.
When is the student reward paid and who is eligible?
Monthly student rewards are disbursed on the 27th of each Gregorian month for regular full-time Saudi students who meet the academic standing and attendance requirements.
I am stressed or struggling. Who can I talk to?
The Guidance & Counselling unit offers confidential one-on-one sessions with professional counselors to support you with academic pressure or personal challenges.
How do I join a club or sports team?
Visit the Student Activities & Clubs office on campus or sign up during the club registration drive held at the beginning of each semester.
How do I make a complaint or appeal a decision?
You can submit a formal appeal or complaint in writing at the Student Affairs office or through the student portal within the specified appeal window.
I lost my student ID card. What should I do?
Report the loss immediately to the Student Affairs office and apply for a replacement student ID card.

Visit Student Affairs

The Student Affairs office is open to every JIC student. Drop by with your student ID and the team will guide you through what you need.
