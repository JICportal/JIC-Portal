Jubail Industrial College
Student Knowledge Portal

Everything that a JIC student is willing to know. JIC Portal is a simple online guide that helps new college students and the ones who are willing to apply for to find practical resources to maintain enough knowledge of our beloved college.

This Portal Will Provide You With:

5 Modules
  • An Overview of the JIC Legacy and its Historical Aspect.
  • Complete guidance of the JIC Departments and Facilities.
  • Primary knowledge of Administrative Hierarchy & College Officials.
  • Important academic Knowledge Sources, Textbooks & E-library access.
  • Complete repository of Official Websites implemented by JIC.
