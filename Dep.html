01 Business Administration Department

About the Department:

The Department of Business Administration strives to prepare students for successful professional careers across various business fields. The department offers a range of programs in areas such as business administration, management information systems, accounting, human resources, logistics and supply chain management, and office administration. These programs provide an educational environment that integrates academic knowledge with practical training, equipping graduates with effective organizational, communication, and technical skills that enable them to succeed in diverse sectors and dynamic environments.

02 Chemical Engineering Department

About the Department:

The Department of Chemical Engineering has established several objectives to fulfill its mission, focusing on creating a supportive and conducive educational environment. This includes developing and implementing interactive, innovative curricula; adopting effective teaching and learning strategies; conducting outcome-based assessments; and formulating continuous improvement plans, all supported by modern, state-of-the-art educational facilities. Beyond the formal curriculum, the department encourages students to actively participate in engineering project competitions, career fairs, scientific clubs, and other extracurricular activities.

Furthermore, the department plays a vital role in advancing the professional paths of engineers, operators, technicians, and other industrial trainees by organizing and delivering specialized programs and courses tailored to the needs of local industry and the community.
